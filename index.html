The company

MINBAL LLC works in music, sound design and audio software. It was founded by Benjamin Balcom, a composer, sound designer, mixing engineer and producer whose work spans records, advertising campaigns and films.

Music and sound design

Original music and sound design for advertising, film and records, for clients and projects including YMCA, Gatorade, Chase, Xfinity, Shea Moisture and PBS's POV.

Selected work: benjaminbal.com

Software

em-ay-dee, Minbal Audio Developments, is the company's software line: small, exact tools for people who work with audio.

The first is NullComing soon, a macOS app that checks a delivery of stems against the mix it came from and reports, in decibels, how closely they add back up.
